# SafeSpace Privacy and Data Breach Procedure

East Central Integrated School · Guidance Office

## 1. What counts as a breach

- Someone who is not the guidance counselor sees a report, case note, or reporter's name
- A lost or stolen laptop, phone, or USB drive that holds SafeSpace data
- A downloaded PDF (case summary or data export) shared with the wrong person
- Someone else gets the counselor's password
- Unusual activity found in the audit log

## 2. Who to tell first

1. The Data Protection Officer (DPO): name and contact in the privacy notice
2. The school principal

Tell them as soon as the breach is found, even if details are still unknown.

## 3. Immediate steps

1. Change the counselor password right away
2. Deactivate any affected student accounts
3. Open the audit log (`audit_logs` table) and check who viewed or exported what, and when
4. Collect and remove any leaked copies, if possible
5. Make a note of every step taken

## 4. Notification

Under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), serious breaches may need to be reported to the National Privacy Commission, and affected people may need to be told. The DPO decides and confirms the deadlines.

## 5. Record keeping

Write down for each incident:

- Date and time found
- What happened and what data was affected
- Who was told and when
- Actions taken
- Steps to prevent it from happening again

## 6. Prevention

- Use a strong counselor password and never share it
- Log out on shared computers
- Do not email or print case PDFs unless necessary
- Review the audit log regularly
- Keep reporter names out of case notes that may be shared
